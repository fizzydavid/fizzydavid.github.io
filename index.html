Junzhao Yang

Ph.D. Student · Carnegie Mellon University

Email: junzhaoy [at] andrew.cmu.edu

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Computer Science Department at Carnegie Mellon University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Richard Peng. Previously, I graduated from the Yao Class at Tsinghua University.

My research interests broadly lie in theoretical computer science. More specifically, I am interested in the design and analysis of fast and space-efficient algorithms, including problems in numerical linear algebra and optimization.
